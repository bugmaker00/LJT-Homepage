Junteng Liu, Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, Junxian He

**Published at EMNLP 2024.** Code repository: Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane.

We discover that there exists a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside the hidden representations of large language models. This finding offers an inner representation perspective on the truthfulness and interpretability of LLMs, and paves the way for new methods to control or detect the truthfulness of model outputs.
