Jinghan Zhang, Shiqi Chen, Junteng Liu, Junxian He

**Published at NeurIPS 2023.**

We propose composing parameter-efficient modules with arithmetic operations, enabling flexible and efficient task adaptation of large language models by combining existing lightweight modules in a compositional, arithmetic manner.
