Junteng Liu, Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, Junxian He

**arXiv preprint, 2025.** Code is available on GitHub.

We present SynLogic, a method that synthesizes verifiable reasoning data at scale to improve the logical reasoning capabilities of large language models and beyond. The generated data is provably verifiable and can be used to train stronger reasoners under both supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning settings.
