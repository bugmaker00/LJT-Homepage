Yuzhen Huang, Yuzhuo Bai, Zhihao Zhu, Junlei Zhang, Jinghan Zhang, Tangjun Su, Junteng Liu, Chuancheng Lv, Yikai Zhang, Jiayi Lei, Yao Fu, Maosong Sun, Junxian He

**Published at NeurIPS 2023.**

C-Eval is a comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models. It spans multiple levels and disciplines, providing a challenging benchmark for assessing both knowledge and multi-step reasoning capabilities of foundation models on Chinese tasks.
