Junteng Liu, Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He

**arXiv preprint, 2025.** Code repository: Vision4Chart.

We study the perception bottleneck of vision-language models (VLMs) for chart understanding, showing that visual features and OCR perception play a critical gating role before any reasoning over charts can occur, and pointing to concrete directions to mitigate this bottleneck.
