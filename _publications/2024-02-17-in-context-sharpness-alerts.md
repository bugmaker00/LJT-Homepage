Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, Junteng Liu, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, Junxian He

**Published at ICML 2024.**

We study in-context sharpness as an alert for hallucination mitigation. By examining the inner representations of models, we show that the shape of the loss landscape during generation (in-context sharpness) can serve as an early signal of potential hallucinations, enabling more faithful and controllable language models.
