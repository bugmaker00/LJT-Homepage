# Junteng Liu

I am a **first-year PhD candidate** at the **HKUST NLP Group**, **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, supervised by **Prof. Junxian He**. I graduated from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024 with a B.Eng. (2020–2024), where I was awarded the **Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship**. You can also find my articles on my [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=tbK9jl4AAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate); for the full up-to-date list, please also visit my [Publications](/publications/) page.

My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**, with a particular interest in **LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning**, **hallucination in vision-language models (VLM)**, and **LLM truthfulness and interpretability**. I am passionate about both building more capable and trustworthy language/vision-language models and understanding the inner mechanisms behind their behavior.

## About me

I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, HKUST, supervised by Prof. Junxian He. My doctoral research (2024–present) is in computer science, conducted jointly at HKUST and with close ties to industry. I completed my B.Eng. at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship. I am broadly interested in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning** — designing better reasoning data and RL procedures to improve the logical and multi-step reasoning of large language models.
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)** — understanding and mitigating hallucinations in multimodal models.
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability** — probing inner representations (e.g., the "truthfulness hyperplane") to understand what and why LLMs say.
